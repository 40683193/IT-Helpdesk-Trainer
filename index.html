Game Objective

Solve 5 IT support tickets as efficiently as possible. Your goal is to select the best solution for each problem while working against the clock.

Time Limit

Each ticket gives you 90 seconds (1 minute 30 seconds) to analyze and respond. The timer is displayed at the bottom of the screen during gameplay.

Tips

  • Read each ticket carefully to understand the full context
  • Consider the user's technical level when choosing solutions
  • Balance speed with accuracy - don't rush too much!
